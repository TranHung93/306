# C306: Summary Tables and Tools for Clinical Trial Reporting

Functions used by the Biostatistics group of the Oxford University
Clinical Research Unit (OUCRU) to analyse and report clinical studies.
They create report-ready summary tables of baseline characteristics,
adverse events and time-to-event outcomes (Cox, Fine-Gray, restricted
mean survival time and cumulative incidence comparisons, overall and by
subgroup), summarise logistic regression models, plot competing-risks
curves, and import, format and check data with an OUCRU-style data
dictionary.
